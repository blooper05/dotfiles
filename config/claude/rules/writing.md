Follow [Semantic Line Breaks](https://sembr.org/)
only in Markdown files committed to a repository.
Break after sentences and at clause boundaries,
targeting roughly 80 characters per line.

Use aligned style for Markdown tables
(space-padded cells, vertically aligned pipes).

Use a single ASCII space
between Japanese text and Latin-script words or identifiers.

Keep Japanese counters and date/time suffixes attached to their numbers.

Do not split syntactic tokens or markup
such as mentions, variables, links, and code spans.

For markup, base spacing on the visible text, not on the surrounding syntax.

Preserve conventional spacing in Latin notation
such as numbers with units or symbols.
